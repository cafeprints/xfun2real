What is a Proxy? ¶

A proxy is a middleman server that fetches web content on your behalf — making it look like the request came from somewhere else. This lets you access sites that may be blocked on your network.

Tab Cloak ¶

Tab Cloak opens sites inside an about:blank tab so the URL bar looks empty to anyone glancing at your screen. Toggle it in Settings. Note: depends on your browser.

Themes ¶

Xfun2 has 12 built-in color themes that change every colored element on the site — glows, borders, buttons, particles, and more. Pick your favorite in Settings.

Particles ¶

The animated star dots and constellation lines in the background can be toggled off in Settings if you prefer a cleaner look or better performance on older devices.

Tips ¶

Tips include a rotating banner at the top of the page, small hints on each card, and a welcome popup on your first visit. Toggle them on or off in Settings.
